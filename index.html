JEPRET

Working title · pre-alpha

A first-person social deduction game for 4 to 12 players. Part horror, mostly comedy.

A crew of cats drives into an ancient Indonesian temple to shoot a documentary. The entrance caves in behind them. And one of them didn't come to make a documentary.

  • Photos are evidence

    Take Polaroids of what you see. The prints are real objects. You can drop them, steal them, or burn them.

  • Someone is lying

    There are cultists in the crew. Keep an eye on who goes off alone, and who comes back.

  • Three seals

    Solve the puzzles around the temple and light all three seals to get out.

  • Ring the gong

    Call everyone together, argue, and vote. Try not to pick the wrong cat.
