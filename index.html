JEPRET

Working title · pre-alpha

A first-person social deduction game for 4 to 12 players. It's cozy and silly, right up until you start wondering who's lying.

A crew of cats rides a pickup truck to an ancient Indonesian temple to shoot a documentary. The gate slams shut behind them. And not everyone in the crew came to make a documentary.

  • Photos are evidence

    Take Polaroids of what you see. The prints are real objects. Hold one up to show everyone, drop it, or pick up someone else's. If one falls into the lava, it's gone.

  • Someone is lying

    One to three cultists hide in the crew. They carry a keris, they can cut the lights across the whole temple, and they can turn invisible for a while.

  • Three seals

    Solve the three seal puzzles around the temple. Light all three and the crew wins.

  • Ring the gong

    Hit the gong to call everyone together. Talk it out, then vote someone into the lava.

  • Talk out loud

    Proximity voice chat. You hear who's close to you, and from which side.

  • Dead, not gone

    Dead cats become ghosts. They can walk around, watch, and talk to other ghosts. The living can't see or hear them.
